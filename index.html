Artistic Philosophy Creative Philosophy

"The best photograph is not taken; it is waited for." "Life happens once.
I frame what stays."

Photography for me is an act of deep listening. It is about understanding the geometry of natural shadows, catching the fleeting gaze before someone realizes they are being watched, and letting the light narrate the unwritten truth of a scene. EC/10 (Epic Captures 10) was born from a desire to break away from sterile, cookie-cutter commercial productions. Filmmaking and photography for me are intimate, sensory crafts—capturing the weight of silence between two words, the cadence of natural light across a face, and the honest texture of human memory.

Whether standing at 14,000 feet waiting for morning alpine alpenglow or tracking shadows across bustling city crossroads, my mission is simple: to make ordinary moments feel like timeless cinema. Operating independently allows me to stay agile and deeply personal with every project. Whether it's a narrative cinema short, an evocative fashion editorial, or an authentic human documentary, every frame is cut with purpose.
