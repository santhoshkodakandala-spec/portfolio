Creative Philosophy

"Life happens once.
I frame what stays."

EC/10 (Epic Captures 10) was born from a desire to break away from sterile, cookie-cutter productions. Filmmaking and photography for me are intimate, sensory crafts — capturing the weight of silence between two words, the cadence of natural light across a face, and the honest texture of human memory.

Operating independently allows me to stay agile and deeply personal with every project. Whether it's a narrative short, an evocative fashion editorial, or an authentic human documentary, every frame is cut with intention.
