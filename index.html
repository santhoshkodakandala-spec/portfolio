Creative Philosophy

"Life happens once.
I frame what stays."

EC/10 (Epic Captures 10) was born from a desire to break away from sterile, cookie-cutter productions. Filmmaking and photography are intimate, sensory crafts — capturing the cadence of natural daylight, the honest weight of human memory, and the unspoken space between frames.

Operating independently allows me to stay agile and deeply committed to every commission. Whether directing an atmospheric narrative short, an evocative fashion editorial, or an authentic human documentary, every frame is composed with intention.
